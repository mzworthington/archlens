# Design system and sandbox

ArchLens uses a custom design language for architecture diagrams and product marketing. The interactive showcase is **Tech, then Design system**, at [`/design-system`](/design-system).

## Identity and drafting grid

- **Drafting grid.** Major guidelines sit at `100px`. Micro subdivisions sit at `20px`.
- **Electric cyan glow.** Active links and highlights use the accent token. Glow is optional.
- **Monochrome contrast.** Navy backgrounds with ink typography.
- **Product naming.** Official names are ArchLens for the company brand, ArchLens Canvas, ArchLens CLI, TraceLens, ChaosLens, AdviceLens, BlueprintSpec, and ChaosSpec.

## Design tokens

- **Semantic colors.** `canvas`, `surface`, `navy`, `ink`, and `accent`. The design token set defines each token once.
- **Node status.** Healthy, warning, and error.
- **Product category accents.** Platform, Intelligence, Resilience, and Contract. The homepage and the docs use them.
- **Typography.** Sans for headings and body. Mono for labels, entity refs, and section eyebrows.

## Marketing patterns

Documented under **UI Components** in the showcase. A button label here is a Call To Action (CTA).

- **Calls to action.** Solid primary, outline secondary, and subtle tertiary. Canvas toolbars keep the glow variant.
- **Product cards.** Full-card links with an icon, a category badge, a tagline, and short body copy.
- **Flow steps.** Numbered cards for onboarding narratives.
- **Call-to-action strip.** A gradient banner with an icon and two actions.

## Vector asset pack

- **favicon.svg** is the brand mark for app chrome.
- **grid.svg** is the repeatable blueprint background pattern.

## Interactive sandbox

Experiment with node archetypes such as web-app, database, microservice, and person. Try status levels, and export YAML fragments.
